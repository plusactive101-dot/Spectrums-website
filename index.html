PRESSURE ABSORBER CORNER BASS TRAPS

Straddling Corner Bass Traps (Superchunks)

All room modes converge into maximum sound pressure at trihedral corners (where two walls meet the ceiling or floor). Deep corner absorption controls sub-100Hz decay times.

Targets: 40 - 150 Hz Essential for Bass
SCATTERING & DIFFUSION QRD / SCHROEDER

Quadratic Residue Diffusers (QRD)

Instead of removing sound energy, diffusers scatter reflections evenly in time and space, preserving room liveliness while eliminating flutter echoes and comb filtering.
